# History: what runs and trials showed

Dated results the method was tuned on. Not needed to change the code; read when a choice in the method needs its evidence. Figures are from the 70-project and 251-project runs of October 2026 unless dated otherwise; settings named are the current ones.

## Why each control panel value is what it is

Settings with no row were set by judgement, not grades (round sizes, minimum counts, `text.shingle_words` 6 since 2026-10-19). "v1.0" means read off the seed-7 grades on the raw score with the probability layer off (2026-10-23); the floor was chosen on the same sheet, so those figures are upper estimates until an evaluation draw is graded.

## Trials not adopted

- **Cheaper decision models** (2026-10-21, US$0.08). On 160 risk-assessment units, Jev 1.13 asked again gave its stored grade on 95 per cent; four cheaper deciders on 53 to 64 per cent, Jev 1.14 on 82 per cent. Too far from a sheet on which Jev's precision was 0.96 to swap without regrading. Gemma 4 31B on 807 graded units: precision 0.70 / 0.74 / 0.78 at recall 0.90 / 0.80 / 0.70, against Jev's 0.65 / 0.70 / 0.74, at about eight times the price.
- **A yes/no pre-filter** ("does this passage concern any digital asset?") kept 99 per cent of paragraphs graded 2 or more while sending 48 per cent on, but cost a quarter of a full asset request itself: less than the gate saves.
- **A one-choice asset question** (which asset, or none) agreed with the person as often as the thirteen graded questions (0.66 against 0.65) at 42 per cent of the cost, but gives no grade. Not taken.
- **The paragraph as context** (PR #48): on an independent sheet the sentence alone ranked better (0.76 against 0.69); with the paragraph the model graded the paragraph and doubled false positives. Yet the person, reading the paragraph, gives an asset the sentence-only question misses (issue #75): `ask.context` is the open test.
- **Splitting the measure question into three yes/no questions**: no gain (0.67 / 0.69 / 0.74 against 0.65 / 0.70 / 0.74).
- **A second pass** on committed units (one-choice asset, delivery mode, conditionality, actor), a scope question and Platt calibration: nothing decided on them; removed 2026-10-22. The second pass had cost US$1.23 against US$0.65 for the other 57,827 units.
- **Per-measure floors on P(grade 3)** read off the v5 sheet (0.3 to 0.6 by measure): sheet s2 mean precision 0.64, recall 0.81; s4 0.61 and 0.69, ranging 0.21 to 0.92 by measure. Replaced by one floor on P(2)+P(3).
- **The paragraph asset map** (every model paragraph graded, 18,834 on 70 projects, US$1.88 for nine assets): replaced by the sentence map on 2026-10-22.

## Definitions

- **v4** (2026-10-20): each measure defined by the intervention, never the asset, with boundaries between neighbours. Before it, climate governance graded 1,300 units committed and about 262 of 680 renewable-energy units were energy-access programmes. The first risk-assessment run had 47 of 153 committed units on the Bank's own concept-stage screening, which the word "screening" invited: a definition is a filter and is tuned like one.
- **v5**: grade 1 adds existing systems, results indicators, economic-analysis assumptions and other projects; a `boundary` column per measure. **v6** (477 graded units): grade 2 takes wording the sentence makes optional; backup power (solar as main supply excluded; precision had been about 0.2) and adaptation applications (about 0.45) tightened. **v7** (331 units, 70 links): physical hardening needs a named engineering measure on a named asset (precision had been 0.40). The v7 run (US$1.06) raised committed units from 1,164 to 2,100, mostly generic sentences; the tightened lines then tested at precision 0.65 / 0.70 / 0.74 at recall 0.90 / 0.80 / 0.70 on 807 units.
- **Taxonomy v2** (2026-10-22): definitions of at most 100 words, one 0-3 scale for assets and measures (committed technical assistance is grade 3), types as overlapping subcategories asked by the fit question. On 131 graded sentences it matched the first draft (risk assessment AUC 0.85, disaster recovery 0.98; US$0.01). The old grades predate the wording: a regrade under it is the real test.
- **Early warning** (2026-10-19): 413 committed units in 39 projects, 287 in one project's restructuring paper reporting a roll-out sentence by sentence. A measure that is itself an asset has a prevalence of 22 per cent in its pool against 4 per cent for risk assessment; "operates" counts every progress sentence; dashboards count projects, not units. Patterns found 7 of the 413, the feedback rounds 137. Results-framework rows read as prose were graded committed, which is why indicators are left out of the search (section 5).

## Corpus figures the parsing rules rest on

- **Parse sensitivity** (local copy): a tighter line gap changes 1.2 per cent of the model text, a looser one 3.7 per cent; without closing component tags at the section change 10,086 paragraphs would be tagged instead of 3,349; word minimum 2 or 4 splits 5.6 or 3.6 per cent of paragraphs differently, no abbreviation list 2.2 per cent.
- **70-project run**: 147 documents (65 appraisal, 13 additional financing, 69 restructuring); 52,025 paragraphs, 14,723 given to the model (7,718 narrative, 3,117 annex, 3,888 footnotes); 970 component rows; 3,349 model paragraphs with a component tag. On 64,323 units, references and fragments were 4 per cent of units and 8 per cent of labelled pools; of 312 sentences a person judged about a measure, the rules left out one and no commitment. 13,749 of 64,292 units carry a lead-in. Indicator rows: 2,064 of 8,697 prose table rows.
- **Procurement**: 2,113 plan documents from 55 projects (9 published none, 6 only empty); 131,610 package rows, 6,886 packages after grouping; component by name 5,173, number only 188, none 1,525; notices 5,909 (4,530 linked), awards 3,485 (2,495 linked). Word repair on 120 hand-checked edits: about 5 per cent wrong, 8 per cent before the fused-pair and short-half rules; "unconsultant" occurred in 149 descriptions, which is why fused pairs are split last.
- **Projects interface** (2026-10-20): version 3 held records version 2 lacked for 81 of 251 projects; the two forms disagreed on the commitment for 30 of 131 projects with both. Several restructurings cut component costs sharply (US$56.5 million to 8.1; 200 to 100) before the interface showed it.
- **Links, first full run**: 13,713 packages, 4,530 with an asset at 0.5 (3,218 of them digital systems); 98,539 commitment-package pairs asked, about one in sixteen linking at 0.5, one unit linking to 49 packages. Pairing on a shared asset group rather than a shared asset made the pairs many; the link now pairs on the asset.
- **Sentence length** (327 graded sentences, six measures): precision at 0.5 flat by length (0.83 to 0.88); recall fell only above 50 words (0.86).
